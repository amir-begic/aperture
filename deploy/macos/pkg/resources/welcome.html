Aperture

An office for your photography business: shoots, clients, invoices with Swiss QR-bill, costs and tax exports.

This installs Aperture for you on this Mac:

  • It runs in the background while you're logged in, and opens in its own window from Aperture in your Applications folder.
  • Only this Mac can reach it. Your data stays in your home folder, in Aperture.
  • It backs up your data every day, and updates itself when you click Install update in its settings.

Installing again later keeps all your data.
